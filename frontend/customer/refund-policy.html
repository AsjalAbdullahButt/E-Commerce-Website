Refund Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

This page is placeholder content generated for development purposes. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be treated as a real, binding refund policy until it is.

1. Cancelling an Order

You can cancel an order yourself, free of charge, from the order tracking page as long as it hasn't yet been shipped (available while an order is pending, confirmed, or packed). Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled — see "Returns" below instead.

2. Requesting a Return

Once an order has been marked delivered, you can submit a return request from the same order tracking page, stating your reason. Our team reviews every return request and will either approve it — restoring the item to stock and issuing a refund — or reject it with a note explaining why.

3. Refund Method & Timing

Refunds for orders paid online (Stripe, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa) are issued back to the original payment method. Refunds for Cash on Delivery orders are arranged directly with you after a return is approved. Processing times vary by payment provider.

4. Non-Returnable Situations

We may decline a return request if it's submitted well after delivery, if the reason given doesn't reflect a genuine product or delivery issue, or if a return has already been requested and resolved for the same order.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please say so clearly in your return request so our team can prioritize it.

6. Contact Us

Questions about a return or refund can be sent through our contact page or WhatsApp support.
