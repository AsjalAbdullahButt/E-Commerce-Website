Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

This page is placeholder content generated for development purposes. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be treated as a real, binding privacy policy until it is.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or contact us, we collect information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and order history. If you check out as a guest, we collect only the email address and shipping details needed to fulfil and track that order.

We do not collect or store your full payment card number, JazzCash/EasyPaisa PIN, or banking credentials — online payments are processed directly by our payment gateway providers (Stripe, JazzCash, EasyPaisa), and we only retain a transaction reference and the payment's success/failure status.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders, including delivery by our riders
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery notifications by email
  • To respond to support requests submitted via our contact page or WhatsApp
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access to your account
  • To improve our products, catalog, and site experience

3. Information Sharing

We share the minimum information necessary with third parties who help us operate the store: payment gateways (Stripe, JazzCash, EasyPaisa) to process online payments, our transactional email provider to deliver order/status emails, and our delivery riders to complete your order's delivery. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention

We retain order and account information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with our legal and tax obligations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us through the contact page.

5. Your Rights

You can view and update your account details, saved addresses, and order history at any time from your profile page. You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page or WhatsApp support.
